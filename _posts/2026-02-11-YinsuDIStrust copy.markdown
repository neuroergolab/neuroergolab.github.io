The NeuroErgonomics labs is heading to Boston for the 2026 Neuroergonomics conference, with presentations from <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/yuxin-cai-072b44293/?locale=en">Yuxin Cai</a>, <a href= "https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeevanjayasuriya/">Jeevan Jayasuriya</a>, and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/spenceleyy">Rob Spenceley</a>.

Tuesday, 7/14: 
 - <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/yuxin-cai-072b44293/?locale=en">Yuxin Cai</a> presents her work, "Modeling Operational Fatigue in Emergency Medical Services Providers Under Real World Conditions Using Wearable Sensors and Machine Learning," as a poster.
 - <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/spenceleyy">Rob Spenceley</a> presents his work, "Are you ready for this? An evaluation of motor-readiness cortical potentials while co-lifting with an unreliable robot partner," as a poster and lighting talk. 

Wednesday, 7/15: 
 - <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/spenceleyy">Rob Spenceley</a> presents his work, "When robots reshape teams: Neurodynamic insights into taskwork and teamwork in search and rescue" as an oral presentation.
Thursday, 7/16: 
 - <a href= "https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeevanjayasuriya/">Jeevan Jayasuriya</a> presents his work "Elevated Cortical Demands in Altered Gravity Tasks," as an oral presentation.
