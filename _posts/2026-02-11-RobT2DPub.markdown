Congratulations to <a href="www.linkedin.com/in/spenceleyy">Rob Spenceley</a> and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/ranjanamehta/">Dr. Ranjana Mehta</a> for publishing their paper on multi-human-robot teams in search and rescue environments. Through this work, they explored how the introduction of a robotic teammates impacts both teamwork, and taskwork of human members. They find that while beneficial in some aspects, robots may introduce "hidden costs" to team members, unobservable with non-neural measures. 

You can read the full paper <a href="https://doi.org/10.3389/fnrgo.2026.1771753">here</a>
