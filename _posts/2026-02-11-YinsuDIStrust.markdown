Congratulations to <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/yinsuzhang/">Yinsu Zhang</a> and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/ranjanamehta/">Dr. Ranjana Mehta</a>, and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/tony-mcdonald-3631b142/">Dr. Tony McDonald</a> for publishing their paper, "The neuroscience of (dis)trust: Proactive takeovers in human-AV interaction"! In this work, they use a functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS), paired with eye tracking and behavioral measures, to understand the neurophysiology of proactive takeover. 

You can read the full paper <a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3820500">here!</a>
